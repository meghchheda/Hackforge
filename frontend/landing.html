How it works

Four decisions, every hour

01

Forecast

Gradient-boosted models predict flows 1, 6 and 24 hours ahead, with conformal bands whose coverage is tested.

02

Size the reserve

Block-bootstrap paths keep outflows clustered. The reserve covers the worst stretch at your chosen confidence.

03

Fund cheapest

For any gap, the ladder ranks overdraft, repo, bonds and equities by total cost, not habit.

04

Sweep overnight

At 17:00 the night-lock sizes the repo sweep so carry beats the risk of the 15-hour lock.

The insight

Borrowing often beats selling

The overdraft costs 12% a year, about 0.00137% an hour. Selling costs a one-time haircut. So for any gap shorter than about two weeks, borrowing is cheaper than breaking the repo early, and the asset keeps earning.
